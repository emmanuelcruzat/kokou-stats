NA Server Winrate Distribution

Every time someone looks up a player on KokouStats, that player's overall winrate and battle count gets recorded. A background crawler also sweeps clan rosters in the background to keep growing this sample on its own. The numbers below are built from that growing sample of players seen on our website.

These numbers are not a true random sample of the entire NA server population, and are probably skewed toward more active clans and more frequently searched players.

Why This Matters

There are a lot of long-standing theories and debates in the World of Warships community about winrates and player skill distribution. By collecting and sharing this data, we hope to provide some insight into these questions and foster more informed discussions. Plus, it's just interesting to see all the data mapped out in one place!
