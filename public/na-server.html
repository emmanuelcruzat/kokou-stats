Why This Matters

There are a lot of long-standing theories and debates in the World of Warships community about winrates, matchmaking, and player skill distribution. By collecting and sharing this data, we hope to provide some insight into these questions and foster more informed discussions. Plus, it's just interesting to see how the players on the NA server we call home are doing overall!
