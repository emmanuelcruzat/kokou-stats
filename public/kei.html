Kokou's Effectiveness Index (KEI)

KEI is a custom rating metric created for KokouStats. It blends a player's win rate with their average spotting damage per battle into a single number, rewarding players who both win games and provide useful information to their team.

The intention is to create a formula to measure player effectiveness WITHOUT using damage.

Formula: KEI = (Average Spotting Damage ÷ 1000) + Win Rate%

For example, a player with a 55% win rate and 25,000 average spotting damage per battle would have a KEI of 25 + 55 = 80.

Rating Tiers

  • Below 50: Bad
  • 50 - 57: Below Average
  • 58 - 62: Average
  • 63 - 67: Good
  • 68 - 72: Very Good
  • 73 - 79: Great
  • 80 - 89: Unicum
  • 90+: Super Unicum
