Statistical Significance Indicator

Each row of a player's Range table is checked against a simple question: is the win rate in that range actually different from how they've played everywhere else, or is it just normal random variation — a win/loss streak that would happen anyway?

Method: a two-proportion z-test compares the win rate inside the range against the win rate in every battle outside that range

Threshold: the standard p < 0.05 cutoff (two-tailed). If the difference could plausibly happen by chance more than 5% of the time, it's treated as noise

What the dots mean

  • Significant improvement (p < 0.05, higher win rate)
  • Significant decline (p < 0.05, lower win rate)
  • Not statistically significant: most likely just a streak
